Follow the money

Is the critical pile-on against Digger coming from outlets owned by tech and oil money? We traced every one of the 60 scored reviews on Metacritic up its ownership chain, then compared each critic’s score with their own track record on 16 similar films.

What the data says The money trail goes cold.

Ownership does not predict the score. Outlets with tech money behind them liked Digger more than average. The one group that fits the theory, oil-linked owners, is five reviews, four of them from a single company.

02 · Testing the theory

Five ways the theory could have shown up

For each test we shuffled the ownership labels across critics 20,000 times. If a gap at least as big as the real one turns up often by chance, ownership isn’t explaining it. All tests use “versus their usual” scores, so a naturally harsh critic doesn’t count as hostile.

03 · The bigger picture

The studio itself changed hands four days after release

If tech and oil money wanted Digger buried, they now own it. Paramount Skydance, controlled by David Ellison and financed in part by Saudi, Qatari and Abu Dhabi funds, closed its purchase of Warner Bros. on October 6.

04 · What this can and can’t show

Where to dig next

What it rules out

A simple pattern where outlets higher up a tech or oil ownership chain scored Digger lower. With 60 reviews, a real effect of about 15 points would usually show up. Nothing close does, except for a handful of oil-linked reviews.

What it can’t rule out

  • Influence that doesn’t run through ownership, such as advertisers, access or editors’ assignments.
  • Private investors we couldn’t see. Some cap tables (Penske Media, The Independent, TheWrap) are not public.
  • Online chatter. This looks at professional reviews, not social media.

Evidence that would change the picture

  • Critics saying an editor changed or spiked a review.
  • Near-identical talking points published across unrelated outlets at the same time.
  • Payment disclosures from accounts pushing the negative takes.
  • The full 319-review Rotten Tomatoes list showing a pattern the 60 Metacritic reviews miss.
05 · Method and sources

How this was built

Scores

All 60 Metacritic critic reviews of Digger (Metascore 45–46). Metacritic converts stars and grades to 0–100.

“Versus their usual”

For each critic we pulled their Metacritic reviews of 18 other films: Iñárritu’s seven earlier features, nine films about oil, tech or wealth (including Don’t Look Up, There Will Be Blood and Eddington), and two recent Cruise films. Their average gap from consensus on those films sets an expected score for Digger. When a critic had fewer than three, we used their outlet’s history.

Ownership

Each outlet traced to its ultimate controller as of October 2026, including this year’s sales of The Telegraph, New York Magazine and Polygon. Tech and oil flags mark only documented money; each critic’s panel shows the reason and a source.
