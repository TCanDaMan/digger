Compiled 2026-10-11. Labels used below: **[VERIFIED]** means sourced to a named outlet or data site (URL given). **[CONFLICT]** means sources disagree. **[BACKGROUND]** comes from the researcher's prior knowledge, not a 2026 source. **[SPECULATION/ANALYSIS]** is inference only.

## 1. Plot, characters, themes (spoilers)

**Basics [VERIFIED]:** An R-rated satirical black comedy, rated R for pervasive language. Runtime is 128 to 129 minutes. Alejandro G. Iñárritu directed it and co-wrote it with Alexander Dinelaris Jr., Nicolás Giacobone and Sabina Berman. The story is credited to Berman, Iñárritu and Jez Butterworth. It is his first English-language film since *The Revenant*. Cinematography is by Emmanuel Lubezki. Tagline: "A man. A plan. A meltdown." Studio logline: "a comedy of catastrophic proportions." Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digger_(2026_film) · https://www.the-numbers.com/movie/Digger-(2026) · https://letterboxd.com/film/digger-2026/

**Cruise's role [VERIFIED]:** Cruise plays two parts:
- **Digger Rockwell**, an aging, balding, overweight Texas oil tycoon who heads Rockwell United.
- **Curtis Markson**, the actor who plays Digger in a stage play called *Fossil Fools*. The film turns out to be a rehearsal and performance of this play.

**Synopsis [VERIFIED, per Wikipedia]:**
1. Digger colludes with the President to drill in Alaska and Greenland and ignores warnings about methane.
2. A gas pocket breaks loose a huge ice shelf, which drifts toward Europe. The men respond with an "information firewall."
3. A prop failure reveals the meta frame: this is a rehearsal of *Fossil Fools*. Curtis's daughter's plane is missing after a hurricane.
4. The ice hits Northern Europe. Before the European Parliament, Digger takes responsibility but **blames global demand**, and the room descends into infighting.
5. A second ice shelf heads for Alaska. The drugged President considers a nuclear strike on China as a distraction, then orders a strike on the ice, which only fragments it.
6. Curtis melts down on stage and condemns the play. Kenny says the play exists to help people process collective grief.
7. Five years later, the new President (Eddie) cuts off Digger's offshore-platform refuge. Digger rants at God, scatters his cat's ashes and jumps into the sea.
8. The theatre audience cheers. Curtis's daughter turns out to be safe. A "Level 4" alert sends the audience, wearing masks, out into a flooded, militarized city.

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digger_(2026_film)

**How the film treats each theme:**

- **Oil and fossil-fuel power [VERIFIED, central]:** The plot is driven by an oil tycoon colluding with the state, suppressing information and ignoring safety warnings. Daily Beast critic Nick Schager lists its targets as "capitalist greed, anti-environmental profiteering, corruption, narcissism." https://www.thedailybeast.com/obsessed/digger-star-tom-cruise-tries-to-dig-out-of-epic-box-office-humiliation/

- **Consumer demand and abundance [VERIFIED, central and contested]:** Digger's roughly 10-minute European Parliament monologue is the film's "spine." It reportedly went through about 70 drafts. In it he calls the audience hypocrites who love oil. Quoted lines:
  - "So let's all just cut the bullshit, because you love oil!"
  - "Do you want freedom? Freedom to bask in the comfort of your unsustainable lives?"
  - "You demand, and I supply."

  Sammy Roth (Climate Colored Goggles) notes that no character rebuts Digger. He thinks some viewers will leave thinking "Tom Cruise has a point." He argues the speech breeds fatalism and that the film shows "little interest in solutions." https://www.climatecoloredgoggles.com/p/digger-climate-change

  Iñárritu has said "we are all part of this madness" and that the film avoids blaming either political side (per Wikipedia).

- **Tech power [VERIFIED, minor]:** Tech is not a main target. No tech billionaire or tech company appears as a character in any source found. The only tech jabs are in the monologue: "You want energy, energy so that you can ask AI to solve the very same disasters that it's fueling!", plus references to phone batteries, TikTok and YouTube. Iñárritu's remark about culture being "Everything is just to get attention, to get likes" concerns attention culture, not tech power. https://www.climatecoloredgoggles.com/p/digger-climate-change · https://www.thedailybeast.com/obsessed/tom-cruise-flop-director-alejandro-g-inarritu-breaks-silence-on-vicious-reviews/

- **[SPECULATION/ANALYSIS]:** The film critiques oil power directly. It critiques consumer demand as shared complicity, through the villain's own mouth, which some read as letting the villain off the hook. It critiques tech only in passing (AI's energy appetite, attention culture). Calling it a film that critiques "tech power" would overstate the sourced evidence.

## 3. Who owns Warner Bros. now (Oct 2026)

**[VERIFIED]** Paramount Skydance closed its takeover of Warner Bros. Discovery on **Tuesday, Oct 6, 2026**.
- Price: about $81B equity, about $111B including debt.
- The combined company is named **Skydance** (NYSE: SKYD). WBD stock was delisted from Nasdaq.
- Leaders: **David Ellison** (CEO) and co-CEO Ynon Kreiz.

Sources: https://www.ksat.com/news/politics/2026/10/06/paramount-closes-on-81-billion-takeover-of-warner-bros-discovery-joining-two-of-americas-oldest-hollywood-studios/ · https://www.bostonglobe.com/2026/10/06/business/skydance-paramount-warner-bros-deal-complete/ · https://www.shacknews.com/article/150919/paramount-warner-bros-discovery-wb-games-skydance-skyd

**Timeline [VERIFIED, AP via WBTV]:** https://www.wbtv.com/2026/10/06/paramount-warner-bros-are-now-one-hollywood-giant-skydance-heres-how-we-got-here/
- **Dec 2025:** Warner Bros. Discovery agreed a studio and streaming deal with Netflix.
- **Early 2026:** Paramount launched a hostile counterbid and eventually offered $31/share. Netflix dropped out.
- **Late Feb 2026:** Merger agreement signed.
- **April 2026:** Shareholders approved the deal. The same month, thousands of Hollywood workers signed a letter opposing it.
- **June 2026:** The DOJ said it would not intervene.
- **July 2026:** 12 Democratic state attorneys general and the WGA sued to block the deal.
- **Sept 2026:** Settlements were reached and approved.
- No Comcast bid appears in this AP account.

**Foreign backing [VERIFIED, AP]:** Saudi Arabia, Qatar and the UAE put in "billions in backing." The FCC approved indirect foreign ownership of up to 100%. The expected stake is close to 50% of equity, with no voting rights.

**[BACKGROUND]** The Ellison family's wealth comes from Larry Ellison and Oracle, a tech company. RedBird is also a Paramount Skydance backer. Re-verify for 2026.

**Timing [VERIFIED]:** *Digger* opened Oct 2, four days **before** the merger closed. It was Warner Bros. Pictures' last release as a standalone studio. The film was greenlit, made, marketed and dated under pre-merger WBD management. https://screenrant.com/tom-cruise-digger-movie-rotten-tomatoes-audience-score-record/

**[SPECULATION/ANALYSIS]** These facts cut against a "suppression" theory:
- The studio that financed and released the film spent about $100M promoting it.
- It ran an aggressive pro-film "decide for yourself" campaign.
- The film's new parent is itself owned by a tech fortune and partly funded by Gulf petrostates. Its new owner did have an indirect interest in tech and oil.

Even so, the new owner took control only after release and after the $8M opening. Sabotaging your own $125M to $180M asset would make little sense. Insiders "at Skydance" did reportedly predict under $40M worldwide (tbreak, relaying TheWrap). That is pessimism about the film, not evidence of burying it.

## 6. "Coordinated campaign", review-bombing and bias claims

- **No reporting was found** on bot activity, review-bombing, astroturfing, or an oil-industry-funded campaign, for or against the film. Searches covered review-bomb, bots, astroturf and coordinated campaigns on Letterboxd, IMDb and RT. The articles that do discuss the split (Screen Rant, HuffPost UK, NME) attribute it to the film's polarizing message and twist, not to manipulation.
